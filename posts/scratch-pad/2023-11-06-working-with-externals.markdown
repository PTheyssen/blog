### What makes this hard?
As a technical engineer I want to solve problems and not push
external contributors to do their job properly (especially in large projects).

Ensure documentation is done properly, conduct knowledge sharing sessions.


Why is working in large teams with externals difficult?


- lack of technical understanding / steering by in-house management, decision makers.
- fundamental nature is to hire externals to build you a software for which
you do not have necessary expertise

- problem if you are not a software company / tech company this is your only real option
