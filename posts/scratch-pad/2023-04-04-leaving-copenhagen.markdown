June 2023 marks the end of my journey in Denmark, Copenhagen.
I came here in August 2021 to complete a Master in Computer
Science at the University of Copenhagen.

How want to review how I changed and what I learned from
this experience.

During the summer of 2021, I had quite different expections
full of hopes, imaginations of how life would be.

Even more the entire world, geopolitics where at 
a different point. But lets not digress.

Why even come to Copenhagen in the first place.

If I remeber correctly some of my thoughts were:

- attending a prestigious university
- try living in larger city (althought this was influenced by my girlfriend)
- try living abroad in a foreign country
- denmark as a rich country, happiness of the people


Highlights:

Meeting new interesting professors, other international students, 
attending cabin trips with other students.
Having nice lectures (SaT, TiPL, CoCo, PMPH, AP, PCS)

Realizations:

I tend to be 

- need social construct -> friends -> people who share interest openly talk about worries
- be more open, share things with others
- plan more adventures
- organize more things with others
- talk to strangers

- problem with being alone -> addictive pull / numbing with videos consumption, still lingering
  explicit videos addiction (brain is damaged -> badly wired)
  
- reflect more on values and how to contribute to various social circles I am participating in
