I really feel like once you start creating more complex engineering / software artifacts you perspective also has to widen and the things you consider while programming.

Economical, societal impact of system

Who uses this tool, for what, what are the expectations of the user.

What is the specification / requirements

Is there a clear notion of specification, requirements you want to fulfill your system should adhere to

Time

  • how will the technology stack evolve (some prediction 5-10 years), still updated, still used, security upgrades?

Tension

How worthit is it to think in such large dimensions while programming if it in reality slows you down from creating that PoC, MVP and thereby increasing time-to-market.
